# PRODUCT.md — Chef James Thew 5-Star Culinary Atelier & Contests Platform

## 1. Vision & Mission
The **Chef James Thew Culinary Portal** is a high-craft, 5-star culinary platform serving Executive Chef James Thew (Grand Palace Hotel Residency). The product bridges haute gastronomy with mass community accessibility, empowering home cooks and fine-dining enthusiasts through:
- **Exclusive Repertoire:** Publishing Michelin-standard recipes across four core pillars: *Juices*, *Vegetarian*, *Non-Vegetarian*, and *Italian*.
- **Freemium Knowledge Model:** Democratizing essential cooking tips and free recipes for the general public, while providing VIP subscribers ($10/month or $100/year) full access to secret recipes, live talk shows, and weekend masterclasses.
- **Global Culinary Arenas:** Hosting seasonal cooking contests with community peer voting, allowing guests and subscribers alike to showcase their creativity and win grand culinary bursaries.
- **Interactive Hospitality:** Facilitating direct dish feedback, photo/video practicing uploads, and transparent announcements of competition laureates.

---

## 2. Target Stakeholders & User Archetypes

### A. General Guest (Home Cook / Culinary Explorer)
- **Mindset:** Eager to discover reliable culinary recipes, improve kitchen fundamentals, and participate in open cooking competitions without friction.
- **Primary Actions:**
  - Browse and search free recipes and kitchen tips.
  - Submit entries to open culinary contests by supplying full name and contact email (no upfront registration required).
  - Cast anti-fraud community votes for favorite competition entries.
  - Review Chef James Thew's background, weekend masterclasses schedule, and official FAQs.

### B. Registered Member (VIP Subscriber)
- **Mindset:** Committed culinary enthusiast investing in mastery, seeking direct techniques, video guides, and community belonging.
- **Subscription Model:** $10 USD / Month (30 days) or $100 USD / Year (365 days, $20 annual savings).
- **Primary Actions:**
  - Full access to 100% of exclusive recipes, cooking tips, technique videos, and masterclass livestreams.
  - Contribute and manage personal recipes and kitchen tips (`My Recipes`).
  - Submit competition entries with pre-filled profile details.
  - Receive real-time concierge alerts and in-app notifications.

### C. Executive Administrator (Chef James Thew)
- **Mindset:** Master curator and executive culinary director maintaining 5-star brand prestige and community vitality.
- **Primary Actions:**
  - Publish recipes with granular visibility controls (`Free` vs `Members-Only`).
  - Create and manage seasonal contests (specifying Recipe or Tip submissions).
  - Review all contest submissions, evaluate community voting tallies, and officially crown winners on the Announcements Gazette.
  - Moderate community feedback, monitor user engagement metrics, and address public feedback.

## 3. The Four Culinary Pillars
1. **Juices & Nutritional Elixirs:** Cold-pressed botanical extracts, cleansing tonics, and restorative beverage formulations.
2. **Vegetarian Gastronomy:** Plant-forward haute cuisine emphasizing local terroir, seasonal vegetables, and delicate herb emulsions.
3. **Non-Vegetarian Repertoire:** Precision protein searing, sous-vide perfection, complex demi-glaces, and classical butcher arts.
4. **Artisanal Italian:** Hand-rolled pasta fresca, saffron-infused risotto mantecato, sourdough crusts, and traditional Mediterranean craft.

## 4. Domain Vocabulary (Curated Gastronomic Lexicon)
- **Atelier:** The private culinary studio and creative sanctuary of Chef James Thew.
- **Repertoire:** The curated catalog of tested recipes and techniques.
- **Concierge:** The intelligent notification and navigation guide for guests and members.
- **Laureates / Gazette:** The official proclamations and winner announcements in the culinary hall of fame.
- **Mise en Place:** Prepared components, verified ingredients, and systematic procedural steps.
